# ArthaMitra

## AI-Powered Financial Digital Twin

Transforming fragmented financial data into personalized insights,
future predictions, and intelligent financial guidance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Problem

Traditional banking applications show balances, transactions and
investments but do not answer:

-   Am I financially healthy?
-   Will I achieve my goals?
-   Can I afford this purchase?
-   What should I do next?
-   How will today's decisions affect my future?

ArthaMitra solves this by building a Financial Digital Twin.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Solution

ArthaMitra combines income, expenses, savings, investments, debt,
financial behaviour, life events and financial goals into a continuously
updated Digital Twin.

It helps customers understand the present, predict the future and make
better financial decisions.
